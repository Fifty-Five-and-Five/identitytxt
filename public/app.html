    How this works

    • Step 1: Research (3-4 min). Fill in your details in the sidebar and hit Generate. The AI searches the open web, reads your published content, and builds a research file. Watch the progress as it works.
    • Step 2: The interview. Once research is ready, hit Start interview. Voice conversation, around an hour. The interviewer already knows who you are, so it skips the basics and goes deep on the things only you can answer.
    • 60-minute time limit. You'll see a countdown once the interview begins. The interviewer will wrap things up automatically as you get close.
    • Going in circles? If the interviewer starts repeating itself, just hit End interview and download your transcript. We can fill any gaps from there.
    • Detail makes the difference. The more you share, the better your AI content will be. Stories, examples, and opinions all help.
    • Already have a research file? Pick Upload your docs in the sidebar instead of running the auto-research.
